Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Thank you for using Clear Easy. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal data remains secure. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our application.

1. About Clear Easy

Clear Easy is a photo and storage cleaning application designed to help users identify:

  • Duplicate Photos
  • Similar Photos
  • Blurry Photos
  • Large Videos
  • Storage-consuming Media Files

The app helps users free up storage space while maintaining full control over their personal content.

2. Photos and Media Access

To provide its functionality, Clear Easy requires access to your photo library and media files.

This access is used exclusively to:

  • Analyze photos stored on your device
  • Detect duplicate photos
  • Detect similar photos
  • Detect blurry photos
  • Identify large videos
  • Allow you to manage unwanted media files

Clear Easy does not upload your photos or videos to our servers.

3. On-Device Processing

Privacy is a core principle of Clear Easy.

All photo analysis is performed directly on your device whenever possible.

This includes:

  • Duplicate detection
  • Image similarity analysis
  • Blur detection
  • Large video scanning

Your media files remain stored on your device and are not transmitted to us.

4. No Upload of Personal Photos

We do not upload, store, copy, sell, share, or transfer your photos or videos to any external server.

Your personal media content remains under your control at all times.

5. No Facial Recognition Data Collection

Clear Easy does not collect facial recognition information.

We do not:

  • Upload faces
  • Create facial profiles
  • Perform cloud-based facial recognition
  • Share biometric information

No facial data is stored by Clear Easy.

6. Information We May Collect

While we do not collect your photos, limited technical information may be collected automatically to improve app performance.

Examples include:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash logs
  • Performance diagnostics
  • Anonymous usage statistics

This information cannot be used to identify your photo content.

7. Advertising

The free version of Clear Easy may display advertisements.

Advertising partners may collect limited device information according to their own privacy policies.

Examples of advertising technologies may include:

  • Google AdMob

Please review the privacy policies of these providers for additional information.

8. Subscriptions and Purchases

Clear Easy may offer optional premium subscriptions.

All payments are processed securely through Apple App Store or Google Play.

We do not receive or store:

  • Credit card numbers
  • Bank account information
  • Payment credentials

9. Third-Party Services

Clear Easy may use trusted third-party services to improve app functionality.

These services may include:

  • Apple App Store
  • Google Play
  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Analytics

These providers may process limited technical information under their own privacy policies.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the application.

Because photo analysis occurs locally on your device, your content remains under your control and is not stored on our servers.

11. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
