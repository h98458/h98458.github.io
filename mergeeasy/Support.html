Common Questions

How do I restore my subscription?

Open Clear Easy and tap "Restore Purchases".

Are my photos uploaded?

No. All photo analysis happens locally on your device. Your photos never leave your device.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
